Current Tenders

At the cidb, we strive to provide you with the best possible local construction industry experience. In this section, you will find our current cidb tenders. We do our utmost best to keep these as up-to-date as possible, allowing you the most time possible to apply for available tenders. Please ensure that when you apply for tenders, all bid documents have been completed in full and with your latest information, enhancing your chance of success when applying. We also ensure that all awarded tenders are announced on our website.

Fraud alert! It is common for scammers to call bidders pretending to be cidb employees and offering to swing tenders your way, for a fee. Do not fall for it. It is a scam!

It has come to our attention that unscrupulous individuals may contact bidders impersonating cidb personnel. They may promise to manipulate tender outcomes in exchange for payment. Please be vigilant and do not engage in such fraudulent activities. The cidb does not endorse or participate in any form of tender manipulation for financial gain.
